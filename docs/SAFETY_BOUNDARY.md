# Safety boundary

Emergence is a defensive forecasting and risk-analysis project. Its purpose is to help people understand risk.

## Never published

- Malware, exploit code, or proof-of-concept snippets
- Credential-theft, intrusion, persistence or evasion procedures
- Instructions for attacking election systems or any other target
- Operational targeting advice, including lists of specific vulnerable organizations or systems
- Step-by-step exploitation of any vulnerability

## The level the site works at

- Threat classes and vulnerability classes
- Public CVEs and public incident reporting, by name and at summary level
- Attack surfaces and system dependencies, described generally
- Defensive indicators and mitigations
- Probabilities and impact pathways

## Elections

The site forecasts strain on election **processes**: disinformation, cyber disruption, official responses. It never forecasts who wins or recommends a vote. Candidates appear only when they are quoted in source reporting.

## Naming organizations

Forecasts are written about **categories** ("a major cloud provider," "a US hospital") unless the question is inherently about a named organization. Named organizations appear in evidence only as they appear in public reporting, with each organization's own response where available.

## Claims and attribution

- Reported claims (anonymous sources, contested allegations) are labeled as reported, not confirmed.
- Attribution to states follows the source's own confidence ("likely," "attributed by the FBI").
- The model's own inferences are labeled as inferences.
